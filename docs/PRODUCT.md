# Product notes

## Tone
Curious, warm, cheeky, never needy or mocking. Indo-English with light Hindi words. Never "test",
"score" or "quiz her". Nothing that feels like an interview.

## Cards
- One tap or one line, under 20 seconds. Skip is always fine.
- Types: this-or-that, pick (Sunday pick), tick-any with "Something else" custom option,
  one line, Never Have I Ever (with request-a-story after both answer), guess the film (emoji),
  bet ("I bet you'd pick"), things I noticed, bug report, try something new (keen/maybe/not),
  Remember When (60 second timer, one line or 30 second voice note).
- Reveal after both answer. Custom options added by one person appear as choices for the other.
- Either person can add their own card (this-or-that or Never Have I Ever).
- "Send a little love" (four emoji) on reveals; a "thinking of you" tap on the home screen.

## Other features
- Right Now: share a photo whenever, no schedule. Compressed on device.
- Movie Night: home or apart, swipe right or left, matches, how-to-watch guidance
  (screen sharing usually goes black for Netflix and Prime; SharePlay covers supported apps;
  Teleparty, Rave, or a 3-2-1 countdown are fallbacks). Check current support before release.
- Micro-Dates, Antakshari Lite, Story Relay, Time Capsule (optional, sealed 3 months).
- Saved: private, silent notes with label, note, date; upcoming-date reminder; surprise idea;
  export to Excel.
- Room data: download everything, keep 4 more weeks (both agree), or erase.
- Room hashtag: chosen together, permanent, cosmetic.

## First-run copy
- Names are typed by users. Ask optional country (for the safety footer) and greeting language.
- Say once: "You can each save things you learn about each other. Saved notes stay private and
  never send a notice, so surprises stay surprises."

## Money
Free. No ads. No paywall. If costs ever bite, a voluntary tip jar at most.
